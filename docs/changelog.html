What's new

Version 0.2.6 · 2026-10-28

  • Fixed: on a phone, a Back in Time (or any choice on the table) could not pick the first card of your timeline.
  • The unstable timeline warning folds into a small badge with a tap, and folds itself while you pick a card.
  • Phone standing up: the card in play steps aside while you pick a card on the table, and a side seat's cards fit on screen.
  • Opponents' plates are no longer hidden behind your plate or the Menu button.

Version 0.2.5 · 2026-10-28

  • Send feedback is now on the title screen.
  • After some matches, the results invite you to share feedback: send it, choose Not now, or ask not to be asked again (Options turns it back on).
  • Profile → Edit name and avatar.

Version 0.2.4 · 2026-10-28

  • Chat: mute any player, and offensive words are hidden (you can turn the filter off in Options).
  • Leaving the game (another app, a locked phone) pauses a match against bots.
  • The screen stays on during a match.
  • Only you can take your seat back after dropping out of a room.
  • A safer Windows app, and a What's new screen after each update.

Version 0.2.3 · 2026-10-28

  • The game starts in your phone's or computer's language, and follows it until you pick one in Options.
  • Options → About: the privacy policy, the terms of use and the open-source licenses.

Version 0.2.2 · 2026-10-28

  • Safer local rooms: the computer that hosts them is protected against bad messages and floods.
  • The Windows app only opens its room server while you play online or sync.
  • Card renamed: Bionic Games.

Version 0.2.1 · 2026-10-28

  • Options → Send feedback: tell us about a problem or an idea, by topic.

Version 0.2.0 · 2026-10-28

  • The first release for everyone: Windows and Android, with automatic updates.
  • Play against bots, learn with the tutorial and the Codex, or play in a local room with friends.
  • Your profile, achievements and the players you met stay on your devices, with no account; move them with a save file or a sync.
  • The phone app plays in landscape and full screen, and every screen fits.
  • French, German and Italian, beside English, Portuguese, Spanish and Japanese.
