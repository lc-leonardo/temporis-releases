Privacy policy

Effective October 28, 2026.

Temporis is a card game made by Lucas Alves, in Michigan, United States ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to information when you play it, on the web, on Windows or on Android. Contact: lucaslopes_leonardo@aol.com.

In short

  • There are no accounts. We do not ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your progress, profile and settings stay on your own devices.
  • We do not track you, show you ads, use analytics or sell anything about you.
  • Information reaches us only when you choose to send feedback.

What stays on your device

Your profile (nickname, portrait, title), your statistics and achievements, the players you met, a match in progress and your settings are kept on the device you play on. Your progress is sealed so that it cannot be edited. A player ID, made from a key created on your device, identifies your save; it is not linked to your real identity.

You can delete all of it: reset your statistics in the Profile, uninstall the game, or clear the site's data in your browser.

What leaves your device, and when

Feedback. If you send feedback (Options → Send feedback), your message, the topic you picked and, if the box stays checked, the game details (version, platform, language, screen size, browser and device model) go to a Google Form that we read. Google hosts the form. Please do not write personal information in a message. We use feedback only to fix problems and plan the game, and we delete it after 24 months, or sooner if it contains personal information.

Update checks. The Windows and Android apps ask GitHub, where the game's releases are published, whether a newer version exists, each time they start. Like any download, GitHub sees your IP address and the request.

Local rooms. When you join a room on a local network, the people in the room see your nickname, your portrait and your chat messages. If "Share my player card" is on (Online screen), they also receive your player card: your nickname, portrait, title, a few game numbers and your public key, signed by your device. Rooms run on a computer of one of the players, not on a server of ours, and the room software keeps nothing once the room closes. You can turn sharing off, and you can forget a player from your list.

Device sync. Syncing two of your devices sends your save between them through a room server, encrypted so that only your two devices can read it.

Save files. Exporting a save creates a file that goes wherever you put it.

Android backup. If Android backup is on for your phone, Android copies the game's save to your own Google account, under Google's terms, so a new phone can restore it.

What we never do

We do not create accounts, collect your contacts, photos or location, use advertising identifiers, track you across apps or sites, profile you, or sell or share your information with advertisers.

Legal bases

Under the Brazilian LGPD, the European GDPR and similar laws: feedback is processed with your consent, given by sending it, which you can withdraw by asking us to delete it; the update check is based on our legitimate interest in keeping the game secure and working.

Where information is processed

We are in the United States, feedback is stored by Google and update checks are answered by GitHub. Information may therefore be processed outside your country, mainly in the United States, under Google's and GitHub's safeguards for international transfers.

Your rights

You can ask us whether we hold information about you, ask for a copy, a correction or its deletion, and object to its use. Because feedback is anonymous, tell us enough to find your message (its date and text). Write to lucaslopes_leonardo@aol.com; we answer within 15 days. If you live in a US state with its own privacy law, you also have the rights it gives you. You can complain to the data protection authority where you live (in Brazil, the ANPD).

Children

The game is suitable for a general audience and collects nothing about anyone unless they send feedback. Children should not write personal information in feedback; if we find a message that identifies a child, we delete it.

Security

The game is built so that it cannot put your device at risk: it asks for no permissions beyond the network, runs only its own code and checks everything it receives from other players. If you find a security problem, write to lucaslopes_leonardo@aol.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version comes with the game and at lc-leonardo.github.io/temporis-releases, with a new date at the top.
